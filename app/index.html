Every tool in one app

  • PastWeatherArchived NOAA radar, hail, rotation and storm reports for any address since 2020, and a case file PDF.
  • NowWeatherLive radar and lightning, watches, warnings and storm arrival times for your place.
  • FutureWeatherGFS, ECMWF, HRRR and NBM maps, drawn on the phone from the raw model data.
  • TropicalWeatherCones, model tracks, recon and satellite, plus every hurricane since 1851.
  • AviationWeatherMETARs, TAFs, advisories, PIREPs, winds aloft, runway crosswinds and route briefings.
  • FarmWeatherSpray checks, work windows, growing degree days, frost, soil and drought for your fields.
  • Property profileHail, wind and tornado history, hurricanes, FEMA flood zone and elevation for an address.
