Forensic & consulting meteorology

Standard Weather analyzes and establishes what likely happened or will happen at a location, and documents it in a certified report you can rely on.

Want a free first look? Run the place and date in the map below and print its summary, then use Request a certified report to send it to us. See notable events for examples.

Add an end date, to total several days

Start here: enter a place, a UTC date and a time. You get the radar at that moment, the day's hail and rotation swaths, storm reports, warnings, lightning, nearby observations, and a written account to paste into a report.

Other ways to use it:

  • Leave the date blank to see the place's hail, wind and tornado history back to 1950.
  • Add an end date to total wind and rain over up to 31 days.
  • Date of loss compares a claimed date with the days around it. Ice & snow shows freezing weather before the time.

Radar and swaths are available from October 14, 2020.

Data sources · not a certified weather analysis

Radar, hail, rotation and rain: NOAA MRMS. Reports: NOAA SPC (preliminary) and NCEI Storm Events (official). Observations: NCEI and the NWS. Warnings: NWS, archived by the Iowa Environmental Mesonet. Lightning: NOAA GOES GLM. Normals and records: NOAA ACIS. Not a substitute for a certified weather analysis.

Forensic and consulting services

Forensic meteorology

Reconstruction of past weather events. We supply certified historical data to establish atmospheric conditions at a specific time and location, for legal, insurance, and investigative use, and support depositions and expert testimony.

Project consulting

Objective atmospheric analysis for operational planning. We review data and models and present the statistical likelihood of specific weather conditions so you can plan with a clear picture of risk.

Our clients include attorneys, insurers, claims professionals, and organizations that need an objective weather record. Read more about Standard Weather.

What a certified report contains

  • The question

    The place, the time window, and what has to be established: hail size, peak wind, rainfall, timing, or whether a storm reached the site at all.

  • The record

    Radar, hail and rotation analyses, station observations and official storm reports, each cited to its source.

  • The finding

    What the data show at the site, stated with its uncertainty, ready for a claim file, a deposition or a court.
