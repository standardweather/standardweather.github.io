Services

Forensic Meteorology

Reconstruction of past weather events. We supply certified historical data to establish atmospheric conditions at a specific time and location, for legal, insurance, and investigative use.

Project Consulting

Objective atmospheric analysis for operational planning. We review data models and present the statistical likelihood of specific weather conditions so you can plan with a clear picture of risk.

What a certified report contains

  • The question

    The place, the date and time window, and what has to be established: hail size, peak wind, rainfall, timing, or whether a storm reached the site at all.

  • The record

    Radar, hail-size and rotation analyses, nearby station observations, and official storm reports, each cited to its source so it can be checked and reproduced.

  • The finding

    What the data show at the site, stated with its uncertainty and documented for a claim file, a deposition or a court.
