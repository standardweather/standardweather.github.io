Privacy Notice

Effective date: November 5, 2026

Standard Weather Forensics & Consulting ("Standard Weather," "we") operates standardweather.net. This notice explains what we collect and how we use it.

1. Who this covers

This notice covers visitors to standardweather.net and people who contact us or join an app waitlist through it. Client case files are covered by each client's letter of agreement, not this notice.

2. What we collect

  • Information you give us: your name, email address, and anything you write in the contact form, plus your email address if you join a waitlist.
  • Technical information: our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, may log standard request data such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request. It uses that data to keep the service secure and running. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser sends your IP address and basic browser information to Google when a page loads.
  • What we don't collect: we don't use analytics services, advertising cookies, or tracking pixels, and we don't sell tracking data.

3. How we use it

We use your information to:

  • answer your inquiry
  • run conflict checks before accepting work
  • scope and deliver services you request
  • tell you when an app you signed up for is available
  • keep the site secure

We use your email for marketing only if you asked to hear from us, and every such email includes a way to unsubscribe.

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent personal information. We share it only:

  • with the service providers that help run the site and our email: GitHub Pages (hosting), Google Fonts (web fonts), FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which delivers contact-form messages to our inbox, and Google (email)
  • when required by law, subpoena, or court order
  • to protect our rights

These providers handle data under their own privacy policies. Information you send about a legal matter may become part of a case file and may be subject to discovery in litigation.

5. How long we keep it

  • Inquiries that don't become engagements: kept as long as needed to respond, and for conflict-check records.
  • Waitlist emails: kept until the product launches or you ask us to remove them.
  • Case records: kept as stated in the client's letter of agreement.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your information, or to remove you from a waitlist, by emailing standardwx@gmail.com. We may keep records we're required to keep by law or for conflict checks.

7. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information, but no website or email transmission is completely secure. Please don't send confidential case documents through the contact form; we'll arrange a secure method.

8. Changes

We'll post any changes here with a new effective date.
