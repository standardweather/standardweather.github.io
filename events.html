Mayfield, Kentucky tornado

December 10–11, 2021. A long-track EF4 tornado crossed western Kentucky that night and struck downtown Mayfield around 9:30 p.m. local time (03:30 UTC on the 11th), part of one of the deadliest December outbreaks on record.

Hurricane Ian, southwest Florida

September 28, 2022. Ian made landfall on Cayo Costa as a Category 4 hurricane in the afternoon, bringing destructive wind and storm surge to Fort Myers Beach and the Fort Myers area. The PastWeather view totals wind and rain over the landfall day and the two days after.

Rolling Fork, Mississippi tornado

March 24, 2023. A violent EF4 tornado struck Rolling Fork in the Mississippi Delta during the evening, about 8 p.m. local time (01:00 UTC on the 25th), and continued northeast across the state.
